Jason Lee

Java Champion • Principal Software Engineer at IBM

I'm a Java Champion and Principal Software Engineer at IBM with over 25 years of experience building enterprise-grade applications. I've been a professional developer since 1997, using a variety of languages, including Java, Kotlin, C/C++, JavaScript, PHP, Python, Delphi, C#, and even a bit of COBOL and JCL. I currently work for IBM on the WildFly/EAP team, where, among other things, I maintain integrations for OpenTelemetry and MicroProfile Telemetry, Micrometer, Bean Validation, and Jakarta Faces.

As President of the Oklahoma City JUG, I'm passionate about community building, mentoring developers, and advancing the Java ecosystem. I am a frequent speaker at our local JUG, as well as at a variety of technical conferences across the country.
